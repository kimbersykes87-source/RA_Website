- **Sleeping Arrangements:** Tents (insulated, spacious), equipped with 120V power. Bring your own bedding, sleeping mat/air mattress, lights, USB fans, or battery packs or personal cooler if you have perishable food/drinks.
- **Community Participation:** Expect to sign up for 1-2 easy shifts (bar, kitchen, recycling etc.) at the Monday meeting. Camp runs daily "Radiance Hour" for sharing skills or stories informally.
- **Bike Allocation:** Bikes provided at Monday meeting; BYO lights/decorations. Combination bike locks provided with the bike.

## Page 7 — Roles / Teamwork
**Image:** `Roster.png`
**Headline:** Teamwork makes the dream work

- **THE HEN HOUSE** — Masters of camp logistics & morale. Lords of everything camp related: allocation of tents, rostering changes, allocation of bikes, gentle prodding of general camp maintenance tasks, general point of call for most camp questions, good knowledge of nearby services and camps, facilitating Radiance Hour.
- **COOK CREWS** — Fuelling dusty souls: put daily allocation out to defrost, warm cooking water totes, clear and setup family dinner tables, prepare serving stations, boil water and cook freezer bags, switch gas off when finished cooking, cooking water returned to totes, clean kitchen - wash and dry.
- **PLANETEERS** — Leave no trace warriors: sweep the camping area, trailers, bedouin tent and art car for empties, drain and crush all cans, empty bins/move to under trailer and refresh bin bags, MOOP sweep all areas, take cardboard to burn barrel camp.
- **BARBIES** — Lubricating dusty throats: get ice for the camp, take recycling to Recycling Camp, clean bar completely, prepare margie mix and coolers, bar shift from 3pm to sunset, clean bar equipment, BYO camp DJ or share the time.
- **ART CAR DRIVERS** — Stalwarts of the deep: must be sober, must hold a driving license, shifts from 2-6 hours day/night, 5 MPH speed limit, drive direct route to playa, responsible for state of charge, responsible for spotters, must be sober.

Our family of like minded individuals cannot be run by a couple of people, a community like this requires everyone to play their own part in the success of the camp. We will each need to sign up to 1-2 shifts to cover the roster. Sign ups will be managed in the Rubber Armstrong Portal App.

## Page 8 — Water

**Headline:** Water is the driving force of all nature
**Body:** Our fresh water totes arrive topped to the brim with 5,000L of filtered drinking water which is more than enough to cover the burner guidelines of 4L litres per day with plenty of contingency for heat-wave refills or those inevitable "forgot-my-electrolytes" chug-offs. ## Page 9 — Radiance Hour
**Image:** `Radiance_hour.png`
**Headline:** Radiance grows when shared
**Body:** As part of our gift to the playa we will have a daily Radiance Hour. Radiance Hour is a pocket-sized show-and-tell where any campmate can beam out a skill, story, or hack. Think yoga flows, sound healing, architecture insights, bike-pimping secrets, turning playa tricks into "default-world" magic, or even the fine art of silly noises. Unless a session needs extra elbow-room in the Bedouin tent, it will commence at the same time as the bar shift. Bring your own props; we'll supply cheers, cushions, and an absurdly supportive vibe. Sign up using the Rubber Armstrong Portal App. Why step up? Because Burning Man is a city of once-strangers who become family, and every gift you share makes our camp burn a little brighter. One quick demo can spark unlikely collaborations and give you that warm post-gift glow before the night even starts. No sign-up sheets, QR codes, or microphones; just a free-flow audience in a super-casual setting. Whether your wisdom is profound or playful, Radiance Hour is your chance to stamp a bit of you onto the playa and help our community shine.

So climb aboard, dance like you mean it, spot like a hawk, and drive like you're part of something that only works because we all give a damn.

## Page 11 — Getting There / RA Express
**Image:** `Rubber_Armstrong_Express.png`
**Headline:** THE ONLY TRAFFIC JAM YOU WILL WANT TO BE STUCK IN
**Body:** Due to limited space and environmental targets we have limited car parking space for you. Please endeavour to carpool where possible and also consider the Rubber Armstrong Express which is a much cheaper way to get to the camp and you can bring more stuff (shopping/bikes) with you. Rubber Armstrong Express tickets are USD 150 return from a Reno Walmart to Playa. Departs Reno at 21:00 on Saturday 23 August. Returns from playa at 11:00 on Monday 7 September. We will touch base in the Whatsapp group closer to the date to discuss the bus and assist with carpooling.

- **Solo mission?** Rubber Armstrong Express at $150. Burner Express at $298 (but no cold beers on board). Hiring a car costs 4-6 times as much once you factor in dust penalties and the $150 vehicle pass.
- **Travelling as a duo?** Rubber Armstrong Express at $150 pp. Burner Express at $298 pp. Rental car - splitting the rental, $567-$711 pp – and one of you has to do the driving, queue for a carwash, and pray the inspector doesn't spot the beige talc in the vents.

Jumping aboard the Rubber Armstrong Express keeps the whole crew together and trims roughly a quarter of the trip's carbon compared with a swarm of rentals; fewer tailpipes, less dust, lighter footprint, bigger shared adventure.

## Page 12 — Contribution / MOOP
**Images:** `embrace.jpg`, `66.png`
**Headline:** HOWEVER SMALL YOUR CONTRIBUTION MAY SEEM; IT IS FELT...
**Body:** We, as burners, are not individually critiqued but rather as a whole. Together we can improve our participation in Black Rock City, justify our placement, and honour those who have tirelessly worked before us to make our nine days of madness an achievable and desirable goal, year after year. Everyone's small but meaningful contributions achieve this combined success. Whether it be helping a fellow camp member clean the solar panels, providing excellent banter on your bar shift, or cooking up a storm with a smile. Everyone contributes in their ways, and we are measured as a collective. Every member follows the ethos Leave No Trace, which is one of 10 Principles of Burning Man. Burners refer to anything that is not originally a part of the land as MOOP (Matter Out Of Place), which includes everything from cans to cigarette ash. As a camp, we make a conscious effort to clean up, not just after ourselves but everyone around us. There will be constant reminders on this. Don't be offended, be informed.

## Page 14 — Tents / Home
**Images:** `_MG_9310.jpg`, `74.png`, `Johnnie_Olivan_-_IMG_20191002_085523.jpg`, `Johnnie_Olivan_-_IMG_20191001_130413.jpg`, `Johnnie_Olivan_-_IMG_20191002_085425.jpg`
**Headline:** Home is where the heart is
**Body:** We will live in temporary homes designed by Rubber Armstrong. The tent's spacious design is inspired and improved upon the successful playa startup "Shiftpod," and is built using triple-layered thermal material. In addition to your tent, we supply a playa-proven, agriculturally designed, aluminium shade cloth that blocks the sun's UV rays while allowing the hot air to escape. If you have opted for an AC unit this will be setup in your tent when you arrive. All tents will have 120v power. The interior is entirely up to you.

We recommend you bring: Foam mattress, sleeping mat or air mattresses (but air mattresses suck); pillows & sheets; warm sleeping bag (some yrs are cold!); light or lamp to hang from central frame; battery packs; USB powered fans (if you don't have AC or didn't request one).

You may want to bring: Storage tubs; clothing rack; floor rug; decorations to make your pad cosy; personal esky / cooler / chilly bin if you have perishable food or drinks that you don't want to share.

## Page 15 — The Bar / Mezcal
**Images:** `bottles.jpg`, `20250518_1519_Mezcal_Harvest_Celebration...png`
**Headline:** Para todo mal, mezcal, y para todo bien, también
**Body:** Rubber Armstrong provides a Mezcal Bar as part of our gift to the playa. Due to our placement this year in one of the city's busiest areas, most of the booze allowance will go towards stocking the bar; our main gift to the playa. We are all welcome and expected to attend the bar, both working shifts and holding down chairs of course. The way it usually works at our camp, and Burning Man as a whole, is that members throw in a communal case of beer every couple of days, share everything they have, and what goes around comes around. The easiest way to estimate how many cases to bring is to multiply your standard maximum number of beers in one night by two, and that should cover yourself and your contribution to your campmates. Any surplus will be gifted via the bar. Please, please, please, no glass bottles! Cans for beers and plastic for spirits. If that is not possible, please make sure you take the glass out with you. i.e. we will not be taking it back in camp vehicles.

## Page 17 — Recycling
**Image:** `giantloo.jpg`
**Headline:** This is not a giant toilet for all your crap
**Body:** Rubber Armstrong provides a clearly labelled recycling system. Everything we consume as a camp is taken out with us when we leave and recycled. With this in mind, please remove all unnecessary packaging from everything you purchase and recycle it before entering Black Rock City. This is extremely important as we have limited space in the camp vehicles, so please limit everything from cardboard boxes to plastic bags. What you bring in, you take out so please make sure you have your own personal trash bag and take it with you on the way out. Sparks Recycling Center, 1455 E Greg St, Sparks, NV 89431 is a convenient pitstop on your way out so you can recycle too.

## Page 18 — Toilets
**Image:** `portaloos.png`
**Headline:** When you have to go, you have to go
**Body:** The camp does not provide toilets because, frankly, we don't shit where we sleep. Banks of porta-potties can be found throughout the city on radial streets on the blocks between C & D and G & H. The exceptions to this are 3:00/9:00, which have banks on D, and 3:00/6:00/9:00 with banks between H & I. There are also banks on the open playa on either side of the Man along 3:00 and 9:00, and out by the Temple. There are some potty banks in deep playa as well. To help you find potties in your time of need, they are marked by solar-powered blue lights above the banks.

## Design reference (art direction to recreate)

**Aspect / rhythm:** original is 16:9 landscape, one idea per page. On the web, translate each page into a full-width **section** (a "page" band) stacked vertically, each roughly viewport-height on desktop and reflowing naturally on mobile.

**Colour palette (from the IDML swatches, CMYK → approx hex):**
- Black `#000000`, Paper/White `#FFFFFF`
- Yellow `#FFF200` (C0 M0 Y100 K0)
- Magenta/Pink `#EC008C` (C0 M100 Y0 K0)
- Cyan `#00AEEF` (C100 M0 Y0 K0)
- Red-orange `#D1341E` (C15 M100 Y100 K0)
- Green `#4CB748` (C75 M5 Y100 K0)
- Deep blue `#2E3192` (C100 M90 Y10 K0)
- Warm orange `#F7941E` (C0 M50 Y100 K0)
It's a bright, high-contrast festival palette on black/white. Confirm exact hues against the PDF.

**Fonts (confirmed from the InDesign export CSS — these are the manifesto's real fonts):**
- **Bebas Neue** — the heavy condensed all-caps display face used for the big page headings (CHEAT SHEET, KEY DATES, CAMP LAYOUT, TEAMWORK MAKES THE DREAM WORK, THANK YOU, etc.). Free on Google Fonts. This is the single most important font for matching the look.
- **Raleway** (Regular / SemiBold / ExtraBold) — sub-headings and most body/label copy. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Minion Pro** (serif) — used in a few places only; substitute a close served serif if Adobe licensing isn't available.
- The `RubberArmstrong.otf` brand face is used only for the logo wordmark, not the body of the manifesto.
Load Bebas Neue + Raleway (self-hosted or Google Fonts) so headings render correctly; without Bebas Neue the pages fall back to generic sans and stop looking like the manifesto.
